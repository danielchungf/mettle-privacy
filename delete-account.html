Delete your Mettle account

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Mettle is a social fitness game developed by Daniel Chung. You can delete your account and its data at any time, by either route below. You do not need to have the app installed to make the request.

Option 1 — In the app

Open Mettle and go to Settings → Delete account, then confirm. Deletion happens immediately; there is no waiting period and no confirmation email.

Option 2 — By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email danielchungfung@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, asking to delete your Mettle account. Requests are handled manually, normally within a few days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account immediately removes:

  • your sign-in account, and the name and email address it provided;
  • your player record — heroes, coins, embers, potions and progress;
  • your daily step and distance totals;
  • your party memberships;
  • your leaderboard entries;
  • your personal reward claim records;
  • nudges you sent and nudges you received;
  • your notification (push) token.

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered, and signing in again creates a new, empty account.

What is not deleted

Shared party records — the parties themselves, their battles, and the monsters a party defeated together — are not deleted, because they belong to the whole party rather than to one member. These records do not identify you once your membership and player records are gone: to everyone else you simply read as having left. If you created a party, it stays with the remaining members and is no longer linked to you.

If you played Mettle before accounts were introduced (version 1.4.3), a copy of your earlier progress may remain in your own Apple iCloud account. That copy is held under your Apple ID rather than by us, we cannot delete it, and it is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Your health data

Mettle reads step and distance totals from Apple Health or Health Connect on your device. The underlying health records never leave your phone — only the daily totals do, and deleting your account deletes those. Removing Mettle's permission in Apple Health or Health Connect stops any further reading.
